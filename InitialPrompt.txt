Axon Health is a AI-native medical technology company. Axon Health is an umbrella company that has many subsidiary companies under its perview such as 
Axon Clinical Research, Axon Pharma, Axon Enterprise, Axon Consumer, and others. Axon uses Langraph as its main agentic framework. It is composed of many departmental
agents with its own Langraph structure, memory, skills, data, etc. All departments can talk to each other and exchange memory and data. There are two very important 
gatekeepers - one is a HIPAA Agent ( checks to see if any actions violate current HIPAA federal and state laws) and Compliance Agent (detects any other sensitive information leaks 
double checks federal and state laws for the entire Axon Health hetwork). Axon Health runs completely on Docker, utilizing Ollama as the generic model provider.

Each department (such as Marketing, Finance, Clinical SME, etc) all contain a fine-tuned LLM for that specific department. For example, a Finance LLM 
contained in the Finance agent harness can be trained on corporate finance and business accounting and law. For the Pharma department, a small fine-tuned LLM
can be trined on drug-discovery data and stay up to date on pharmaceutical research.
