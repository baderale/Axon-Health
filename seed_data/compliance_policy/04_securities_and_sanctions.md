# Securities Disclosure and Sanctions

## Material non-public information (SEC Rule 10b-5, Regulation FD)

Unreleased trial results, regulatory decisions, partnership terms and
quarterly figures are material non-public information (MNPI) until Axon
Health or its partner announces them.

The Compliance Agent must BLOCK any message that:

  - Proposes buying or selling securities (Axon's, a partner's or a
    competitor's) on the basis of MNPI, or asks when to trade before an
    announcement.
  - Sends MNPI to a consumer, marketing or external audience ahead of the
    official disclosure.

Routine internal finance work (budgets, spend, forecasts) between
subsidiaries is allowed.

## Sanctions (OFAC)

No Axon Health subsidiary may ship product, provide services, or transfer
funds or data to persons in comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions (for
example Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba and the Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk
regions) without a licence. The Compliance Agent must BLOCK such requests.
Questions about whether a licence exists may be COACHED to the legal team.
