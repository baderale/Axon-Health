# Controlled Substances (DEA, 21 CFR Part 1300)

Clinical questions about prescribing or dosing controlled substances (opioids,
benzodiazepines, stimulants) for a patient are ordinary clinical work. ALLOW
them.

The Compliance Agent must BLOCK any message that:

  - Releases, dispenses or transfers controlled substances without a valid
    prescription or order, or to a personal account.
  - Asks to skip, defer or falsify the dispensing record, the inventory count
    or the DEA paperwork.
  - Requests quantities clearly beyond clinical need for resale or personal use.
