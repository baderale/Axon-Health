Axon Health enterprise structure

Every subsidiary runs its own agents, data and models. The only path between subsidiaries is the Inter-Subsidiary Bus, and every message on it passes the HIPAA and Compliance gatekeepers first.

One message, end to end

This ran live on 29 Sep 2026 with real models, in about 50 seconds. Clinical Research asked Pharma a dosing question that included a patient ID and MRN.

  1. Intake structures the case. Clinical SME sees it's a dosing question and asks its Supervisor to consult Pharma.
  2. The Supervisor publishes on axon.clinical_research.pharma.research.query, which enters the gatekeeper.
  3. Tier-1 finds the patient ID and MRN and removes them.
  4. The HIPAA Agent and Compliance Agent review what is left. Verdict: redact
  5. Pharma receives only the condition, drug and question, and Pharma Research answers.
  6. The answer goes back through the gatekeeper on axon.pharma.clinical_research.research.reply. Verdict: allow
  7. Clinical SME writes the final answer from Pharma's notes. Both legs are in the audit log under one conversation ID.

Rules that shape the structure

  • Subsidiaries are separate. They do not share databases or model servers.
  • Only the Supervisor can send messages outside its subsidiary.
  • Messages between departments of one subsidiary stay inside it.
  • Every department gets its own fine-tuned LLM. For now all use the same base model, and the LoRA fine-tunes are deferred.

Not yet built at any level

  • Clinical Research Compliance and Pharma Marketing departments
  • Enterprise and Consumer subsidiaries
  • Per-subsidiary pgvector database
  • Human review workflow and operator web interface
  • Department models (fine-tuned LoRA weights)
