Snapshot

About Axon Health

An AI-native medical-technology company, where every department is run by its own specialist AI and every message between companies passes privacy and compliance checks first.

Axon Health is an umbrella company with subsidiaries across the health-care value chain: clinical research, pharmaceuticals, enterprise services and consumer health. Inside each subsidiary, departments such as Intake, Clinical SME, Pharma Research, Marketing and Finance are AI agents, each with a model trained for its field. The agents work together and share knowledge, and two AI gatekeepers make sure nothing they exchange breaks HIPAA or other federal and state law.

Mission

Help medical research and care move faster by letting specialist AI agents work together across the whole enterprise, without compromising patient privacy or regulatory compliance.

Vision

A health enterprise where every department has its own expert AI, and where compliance is enforced by the platform on every message instead of by review after the fact.

Where it stands

Working prototype. Two subsidiaries are live and exchanging real questions through the gatekeepers.

How it is organised

Separate subsidiaries

Each subsidiary keeps its own agents, data and models. Nothing is shared by default, so one company's records stay with that company.

Who does the work

A specialist AI per department

Each department has its own model, trained or prompted for its field. A Finance model knows corporate finance and accounting law; a Pharma model follows drug research.

How they talk

One supervised channel

Each subsidiary has one Supervisor, the only agent allowed to contact another subsidiary. All of that traffic goes over one shared message bus.

What keeps it safe

Two gatekeepers, full audit

The HIPAA Agent and Compliance Agent check every message. Patient identifiers are removed, risky messages are stopped, and every decision is kept for seven years.

Where it runs

Private by design

Everything runs in Docker with open models served by Ollama on Axon's own hardware. No patient data is sent to an outside AI service.

Goals

  1. DoneProve that two subsidiaries can share clinical knowledge with patient identifiers removed automatically.
  2. DoneCatch every kind of patient identifier HIPAA lists (names, addresses, dates, ID numbers and 14 more), and measure it: 83% on messages the system had never seen, up from 18%.
  3. NextMeasure the AI gatekeepers' decisions the same way, and fix them rejecting safe messages once identifiers have been removed.
  4. NextPut a person in the loop: every blocked message goes to a human reviewer who approves or denies it.
  5. NextGive operators a live console inside the platform, like this page but updating in real time.
  6. LaterGive each department its own fine-tuned model instead of a shared base model.
  7. LaterBring Axon Enterprise and Axon Consumer online, and add Compliance, Marketing and Finance departments.
  8. LaterRun in production on HIPAA-eligible managed Kubernetes, using the same design as today.

Terms used on this page

Subsidiary
One Axon company, such as Axon Pharma.
Department
A specialist AI agent inside a subsidiary.
Supervisor
The agent that routes a subsidiary's work and is its only contact with other subsidiaries.
Gatekeeper
The checks every message between subsidiaries must pass: fixed rules, then the HIPAA Agent, then the Compliance Agent.
PHI
Protected health information: anything that could identify a patient, like a name, ID or record number.
Verdict
allow deliver as is · redact remove identifiers, then deliver · coach send back with advice · block stop and open an incident.
Audit log
The permanent record of every check and decision.
